In short: TradeWind has no login, no checkout, and never asks for payment details. We use standard analytics and advertising cookies (including Google AdSense) and affiliate tracking links (including Amazon Associates) — both of which are common, disclosed uses of browser cookies.

Overview

TradeWind ("we", "us", "our") operates tradewind-sigma.vercel.app (the "Site"), a curated affiliate showcase of tech gadgets. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and used when you visit the Site. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Data we collect

Because TradeWind has no accounts, no checkout, and no forms that collect payment information, the data we collect is limited to standard technical and analytics data, such as:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, referring URL, approximate location (derived from IP), device and browser type.
  • Cookie identifiers — set by our advertising and affiliate partners (see below), not by TradeWind directly.
  • Voluntarily provided information — if you email us via the Contact page, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

We do not collect names, addresses, or payment card details anywhere on this Site, because no such fields exist here.

Cookies & similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. TradeWind and its advertising/affiliate partners use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and pixel tags) to:

  • Remember basic site preferences
  • Measure aggregate traffic and page performance
  • Attribute purchases made after clicking an affiliate link back to this Site, so we can be paid a commission
  • Serve and measure relevant advertising

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect affiliate commission attribution and may make some parts of linked third-party sites behave differently, but it will not prevent you from browsing TradeWind itself.

Advertising — Google AdSense

This Site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's own Partner Sites policy.

Affiliate link tracking

Every "View on Official Store" button on this Site is an affiliate link, including links participating in the Amazon Associates Program and other affiliate networks. When you click one, the destination retailer's own cookie or tracking parameter records that the visit came from TradeWind, so that we may be credited with a commission if you make a qualifying purchase — at no extra cost to you. TradeWind does not receive your payment or shipping information through this process; that data stays entirely with the retailer.

Third-party sites

Every product listing links out to a third-party retailer or brand website. Once you leave TradeWind, that site's own privacy policy and terms apply — we encourage you to review them, since we do not control and are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external sites.

How data is shared

We do not sell personal information. Aggregate, non-identifying usage data may be shared with:

  • Analytics providers, to understand site traffic
  • Advertising partners (Google AdSense), to serve and measure ads
  • Affiliate networks (Amazon Associates and similar), to attribute referred purchases
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers (Vercel), as necessary to operate the Site

Your choices & rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California). Because TradeWind itself stores very little personal data, most such requests are best directed to the specific advertising or analytics provider (see the opt-out links above), or to us via the Contact page for anything we do hold, such as emails you've sent us.

Children's privacy

TradeWind is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent via our Contact page.
